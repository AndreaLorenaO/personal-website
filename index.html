About

My professional journey began in the field of geology, where I learned to observe patterns, interpret data, and look beneath the surface, literally and figuratively. Working in field research taught me the value of precision and attention to detail, analitical and critical thinking, and most importantly how to translate complex information into meaningful insights. If you are curious about my scientific background, you can explore my published papers on Google Scholar.

As my career evolved, I became interested in development, initially as a tool to enhance my geological work. But along the way, I discovered how exciting it was to build digital solutions from scratch. Learning object-oriented languages for backend development showed me that the same analytical mindset and problem-solving skills I used in science could also be applied to technology.

Over time, my curiosity grew beyond writing code, I became fascinated by the thinking process behind how digital solutions are conceived, designed, and brought to life. That curiosity naturally led me toward design and user experience.

Today, as a UX/UI designer and researcher, I combine all these perspectives—scientific thinking, analytical problem-solving, and human-centered design—to create products that not only look good but make sense for the people who use them and the business. My approach is deeply rooted in research, empathy, and iteration, always aiming to turn user needs into clear, intuitive experiences. I invite you to explore my awesome work.
